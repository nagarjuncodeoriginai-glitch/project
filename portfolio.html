Our roadmap combines cutting-edge research with practical product development.

Q3 2026 - Near Term

MetaHuman-Quality WebGL Avatar

Replace 2D canvas with full 3D WebGL avatar using custom shader pipeline. Realistic skin subsurface scattering, hair strand rendering, and dynamic lighting. Support for multiple avatar personas (male/female/custom).

Q4 2026 - Mid Term

Multi-Modal AI Integration

Vision understanding (describe what camera sees), multi-language support (Hindi, Tamil, Bengali via Krutrim), emotion-aware responses that adapt conversation style in real-time. Memory persistence across sessions.

Launch as a SaaS platform: customizable AI companions for education, healthcare, customer service, and personal productivity. AR/VR integration for Apple Vision Pro and Quest. Voice cloning and custom personality training.
